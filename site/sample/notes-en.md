This app works, but several of its problems could be used against it today. Fix **the OpenAI key** first: it is in a variable that ships to every visitor's browser, so anyone can copy it and spend your money. Right after that, take out **the agent route that runs the AI's answer as a command**, because it lets a stranger run anything on your server. The invoice query, the PDF download and the link preview each trust what the visitor sends, and the old `next` and `axios` versions need an update.
